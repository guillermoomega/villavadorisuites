A place where time stands still

In the heart of the Córdoba mountain valleys, just 30 minutes from Córdoba Capital, there is a place where time stands still.

Villa Vadori Suites is an adults-only property of 8 carefully designed units, surrounded by native woodland, silence, and a park with a stream and pool that invite you to do nothing... or everything.

Just steps from Gran Vadori, an award-winning gourmet criollo restaurant with 15 years of history, every stay is a complete experience where rest and good food come together naturally.

Spaces to enjoy

Beyond your unit, Villa Vadori Suites offers spaces designed so the experience doesn't end when you close the door.

The park

A central park where nature and design coexist in harmony. A 50-meter artificial stream surrounds a beach-style pool, creating a unique setting where water, greenery, and mountain silence become the stars of your rest.

The fire pit

The social fire pit is where conversations run long and time stops mattering. Surrounded by benches with native woodland as a backdrop, it's the ideal space to end the day the way it deserves.

The grills

For those who prefer the ritual of fire in private. We have group grills and grills for couples, so an Argentine asado can be part of your mountain experience.

The pool

A beach-style pool surrounded by a 50-meter artificial stream. The ideal spot to cool off, sunbathe, and enjoy the water with the calm of a private setting amid mountain nature.

The lounge

We have a lounge dedicated to VVS guests — a place to read, play, gather, or use for trainings and workshops.

Group experiences and activities

Villa Vadori Suites is the ideal starting point for a range of activities in the Córdoba mountains.

Cyclists and hikers

The property sits at the start of the road to the Astronomical Observatory, one of the most popular routes in the area. A very low-traffic rural road, ideal for bike rides or walks, plus a natural trail along the stream suitable for trekking.

Motorsport enthusiasts

Just 8 km via an internal road from the Cabalén Racetrack, Villa Vadori Suites is the perfect base for anyone coming to experience motorsports in Córdoba. Covered parking available.

Motorcycles and classic cars

Low-traffic mountain roads, unique landscapes, and covered parking. The perfect setting for groups who make the road itself part of the experience.

Trainings and workshops

A complete solution for workdays, trainings, or workshops: lodging for up to 20 people and award-winning cuisine just 80 meters away. All on one property, surrounded by mountain nature.

Meditation / mindfulness / wellness retreats

The mountain setting and privacy are ideal for meditation retreat groups or holistic practices amid nature.

Frequently asked questions

Is Villa Vadori Suites adults-only? +

Yes, we welcome guests over 15 years old. Maximum capacity is 16 guests across 8 units.

Where is it located? +

In Falda del Carmen, Sierras de Córdoba, Argentina — 30 minutes from Córdoba Capital, 20 minutes from Villa Carlos Paz, and 40 minutes from Villa General Belgrano.

What types of units do you have? +

4 standard suites of 20 m² with a private terrace and stream views, and 4 premium suites of 24 m² with greater privacy and independence. All include heating/cooling air conditioning, WiFi, a 42" Smart TV, and daily housekeeping.

How much does a night cost? +

Reference rates in low season, for 2 adults:

  • 1 night with breakfast: from $140,000 ARS (standard suites) / $180,000 ARS (premium suites)
  • 1 night with breakfast + lunch or dinner at Gran Vadori: from $220,000 / $260,000 ARS
  • 2 nights with breakfast + 2 lunches or dinners: from $360,000 / $430,000 ARS

Rates vary by season and current promotions. Check updated availability via WhatsApp.

What time are check-in and check-out? +

Check-in from 2:30 PM and check-out until 10:30 AM. On Sundays, late check-out is available as part of weekend packages.

Is breakfast included? +

Yes. From Thursday to Sunday and on holidays it's served at Gran Vadori from 9 to 11 AM: espresso, tea, homemade bread toast, artisanal jam, scrambled eggs, cream cheese, and roasted ham. On other days, a dry breakfast is provided in the suite.

Can you dine at Gran Vadori during your stay? +

Yes. Gran Vadori is 80 meters from the units — an award-winning gourmet criollo restaurant with 15 years of history, open Thursday to Sunday for lunch and Saturdays also for dinner.

How do you make a reservation? +

Reservations are handled exclusively via WhatsApp at +54 9 351 642-7600.

Are pets allowed? +

No, Villa Vadori Suites does not allow pets.

What is the housekeeping service like? +

Daily housekeeping is provided in all units. Sheets are changed on the fourth night of the stay, unless an extra change is requested.

What activities are nearby? +

The property sits at the start of the road to the Astronomical Observatory, ideal for cycling and hiking, and has a trail along the stream for trekking. It's 8 km from the Cabalén Racetrack via an internal road. It also hosts motorcycle and classic car groups, meditation and wellness retreats, and corporate trainings or workshops for up to 20 people.
